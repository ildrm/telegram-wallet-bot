# Independent application-security and support assessment

Assessment scope: the executable Laravel sandbox, private Telegram UX, database financial boundary, fake custody, operational controls and regulated-custody implications. This document is an engineering review, not an independent external security audit, legal opinion or approval to hold customer assets.

## Concrete trust boundaries

1. Telegram controls numeric account identity and delivers updates through a secret-authenticated HTTPS webhook. A stolen Telegram account grants that user's wallet access; this bot offers no independent customer authentication factor. Telegram messages are not a suitable secret-storage channel.
2. The inbox, actor locks, callback ownership/expiry and single-use consume operation protect bot workflows. Financial services independently enforce business rules and administrator permissions. UI hiding is never authorization.
3. Only ledger journals/postings determine balances. Immutable database triggers reject updates/deletes of posted history/audit records. Application transactions lock controls, affected users/accounts and quotes; compensating journals preserve history. Database administrators can still replace schema/triggers or restore malicious data, so privileged database access remains a critical trust boundary.
4. The custody adapter owns address verification, provider authentication/idempotency and chain finality. The included adapter is a persistent simulator, never a real signer, provider integration or chain oracle.
5. The transactional outbox/database queue separates committed financial effects from HTTP delivery. A notification retry must not invoke a new financial operation. Provider calls run outside financial/inbox DB transactions; request identifiers survive uncertain outcomes.

## Operations and recovery constraints

An administrator may immediately pause new transfers/withdrawals and Compliance may freeze a wallet or ledger-hold funds, each via a fresh owned callback. Pausing is not proof that an already submitted chain transaction was canceled. Unknown withdrawal outcomes stay reserved until authoritative reconciliation; never resend under a new key or manually edit ledger rows. Reorganizations, negative customer balances, custody/backing discrepancies and unrecognized deposits require finance/compliance review. A reconciliation discrepancy automatically pauses transfers; an authorized human investigates before resuming.

Support uses private ticket conversations; staff never request a private key, recovery phrase, bot token or custody credential. A report of account takeover should trigger authorized freeze and incident escalation, preserving records. This MVP has no independently verified account recovery process; never rebind an account on a username or support claim. Support operators can view only their authorized cases through their role; a customer's tickets remain owner-scoped. The current queue/thread views are intentionally bounded; full transcript export and lifecycle management require implementation before operational scale.

Failed bot delivery after a successful payment is a notification incident. Check the owned transaction record and outbox/worker failure, retry only the outbox entry, and tell users to refresh history. Failed provider submission with an uncertain outcome is a financial incident. Keep the reservation, query the original provider reference/idempotency key, escalate if no authoritative answer, and do not promise completion or refund before evidence.

Treat a changed owner payout destination as high risk even though this bot has no mutation route. Deployment-level configuration is privileged and must undergo two-person review, delay, provider allowlisting and audit. Pending proposals check the configured destination again and do not silently follow a new address. Never present a stale reconciliation snapshot as spendable owner revenue.

## Compliance and privacy work that remains blocking

Qualified counsel must identify operating entities, customer jurisdictions, custody/virtual-asset service licensing and registration, AML/KYC/customer due diligence, sanctions screening, suspicious-activity reporting, Travel Rule obligations, consumer disclosures, transaction monitoring, funds safeguarding, insolvency treatment and tax/accounting duties. None is satisfied by an `I am eligible` button. Determine whether the exclusively Telegram interface can lawfully and securely collect required identity/Travel Rule data; if it cannot, the product cannot launch under this interface constraint. Do not collect documents or sensitive verification data in ordinary bot messages as an improvised workaround.

A privacy specialist must approve legal bases, notices, processor/provider agreements, data residency and cross-border transfer rules, data subject access/deletion procedures, mandatory financial retention and audit exceptions, breach notification and sensitive support-data handling. Telegram's own processing, account takeover and notification visibility must appear in customer disclosures. Current sandbox text is a disclosure placeholder, not approved production terms.

## Security no-go conditions

Real customer funds remain prohibited until an independent application/infra security assessment, custody-provider assessment and qualified regulatory approval are complete, with resolved critical findings. Additionally require the verified real adapter and provider credentials policy; real MySQL concurrency/crash and backup-restore replay evidence; a separate customer authentication/recovery/risk design; production KYC/sanctions/Travel Rule feasibility; reconciled custody and financial controls; monitored queue/outbox/provider incident drills; audited admin/configuration governance and an adequately staffed support process. Passing the sandbox's automated tests does not remove these blockers.
